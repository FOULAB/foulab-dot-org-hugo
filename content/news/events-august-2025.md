### ESPHome workshop - Saturday August 30th @ 13:00 - 18:00

ESPHome is an open-source firmware framework that lets you turn your esp32 based project into reality and integrate it in home assistant.

It is aimed at beginner users that want to have a first hand experience with it and get started on doing cool projects that are accessible to non programmers.

The workshop will show: installing ESPHome in home assistant, setting the wifi variables and connecting a esp32-c3 to a bme280 sensor(temperature, humidity and pressure) and writing the firmware using ESPHome yaml config file.

-Hardware kit is 20$, donation to Foulab to cover expenses. Includes esp32c3, bme280, jumper cables and it's yours to keep after the workshop.
-Required, a laptop + usb-c cable to write your config file and doing the initial flashing of the esp32.
-Attendance is free but priority will be given to those with a hardware kit. 

*Hosted by photon303*
