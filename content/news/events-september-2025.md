The weather might be starting to cool off --- but we're just warming up! Check out these events for the month of September:

### Workshop Nights - Mondays @ 19:00 - 23:30

Got a project that would be too loud to work on during Open Nights? Need to use the power tools in the workshop? Drop by on Mondays and work on your projects, help organise the lab and workshop, or just hang out.

*Hosted by li-ion*

<br/>

### Mesh Night - Wednesday September 3rd @ 19:00 - 22:00

Curious about alternative network topologies? Don't know what a network topology is, but ecstatic even just considering the idea of a different internet, made up of physical nodes throughout Montreal?

The monthly Mesh Night has returned! Come hang out and discuss mesh networking, including Meshtastic, Reticulum, and more!

*Hosted by photon303*

<br/>

### No Net Night - Tuesday September 9th @ 18:30 - 23:30

No WAN, all LAN. We'll turn off our connection to the Internet for the evening. Come hang out, work on off-line projects, or explore the Foulab internal network and various curiosities around the lab.

<br/>

### *Hackers* 30th Anniversary Celebration - Tuesday September 16th @ 20:00 - 22:00

Join us to hack the planet and celebrate the iconic movie *Hackers*, which was released 30 years ago.

<br/>

### Home Assistant Night - Wednesday September 17th @ 19:00 - 22:00

Interested in home automation? Join us to learn about and share experiences with Home Assistant and other home automation technologies.

This event will become a monthly occurrence.

*Hosted by photon303*

<br/>

### Foulab Monthly Meeting - Tuesday September 23rd @ 20:00 - 21:30

As is tradition, we'll discuss Foulab business, updates on various projects in the Lab, and plans for the upcoming months. Membership not required to participate.

<br/>

### Fediverse Night - Wednesday September 24th @ 19:00 - 22:30

Whether you're a developer of fediverse software, an instance admin, a user, or just fedi-curious, join us for a night of discussion about decentralised social media.

*Hosted by n00q*

### Killed by Dice Presents: Fantasy for Real - Sunday September 28th @ 16:00 - 19:00

Fantasy for Real is a light, whimsical fantasy role-playing game by Ron Edwards.

der_moderne_man will be hosting the game at Foulab on Sunday, September 28, 2025 from 16h00 to 19h00.

Beginners are welcome and all materials necessary for play will be provided.

Please contact `david AT killedbydice.com` to reserve a spot.

### National Day for Truth and Reconciliation Fundraising - Tuesday September 30th @ 18:30 - 23:00

Foulab recognizes National Day for Truth and Reconciliation. In honor of
this day, we will be raising awareness and funds starting on 
September 30th. To this effect, we will be pressing buttons to spread
the word -- people are invited to make a donation in exchange for a button.
We will keep the donations going until October 7th. All donations will
go to the [Native Friendship Centre of Montreal](https://www.nfcm.org/).

More information about the National Day for Truth and Reconciliation
can be found at the [National Centre for Truth and Reconciliation](https://nctr.ca/).
