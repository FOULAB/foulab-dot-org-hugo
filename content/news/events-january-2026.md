At the turn of the year, one of us joked that they hope 2020 part 6
will be more positive. We do our part at Foulab and there are lots
of positive vibes to be found at our events this month.

<br/>

### No Net Night - Tuesday January 6th @ 18:30 - 23:00

New Year, new No Net Night! Once a month we turn off the Internet at
Foulab to see what life without it is like. People tend to get 
creative and there's lot of activities and resources to enjoy at
Foulab that don't involve the Internet.


### Mesh Night - Wednesday January 7th @ 19:00 - 22:00

On this night, we discuss and experiment with emerging mesh networking 
technologies like Meshtastic and Reticulum. It is also a good chance
to learn about it for the first time if you are curious.

Mesh Nights are a recurring monthly event, hosted on the first Wednesday of each month.

*Hosted by photon303*

<br/>

### Foulab Monthly Meeting (2026 Election Edition) - Tuesday January 20th @ 20:00 - 21:30

We will be having our monthly meeting on the open night of Tuesday,
January 20th. Monthly meetings are where we discuss Foulab business.
They are open to the public and are a good opportunity to learn about
how Foulab operates and get involved.

This month is our annual election for Foulab officers. The members
will be voting for the positions of President, Vice-President, 
Treasurer, and Secretary.

<br/>

### Home Assistant Evening - Wednesday January 21st @ 19:00 - 22:00

Come learn and talk about Home Assistant. Check out our installation
and tell us about yours!

Home Assistant nights are hosted each month on the third Wednesday of the month.

*Hosted by photon303*

<br/>

### Intro to Cryptocurrency - Thursday January 22nd @ 20:00 - 22:00

Intro to cryptocurrency and blockchains. Brainstorming and discussion.

*Presentation by Alban*

<br/>

### Fediverse Night - Wednesday January 28th @ 18:30 - 23:00

Whether you’re a developer of fediverse software, an instance admin, a user, or just fedi-curious, join us at the monthly Fediverse Night for discussion about decentralised social media.

This is a recurring thing, hosted on the last Wednesday of each month.

*Hosted by n00q*

<br/>

...

As usual, January events are all open to the public and free to attend,
although we do appreciate donations.

Tuesdays and Thursdays are Open Nights. They are a great time for 
newcomers to visit the space. Sometimes events also happen on the 
these nights.

Mondays are our Workshop Nights. On these nights people make noise
in the workshop and help with its organization.
