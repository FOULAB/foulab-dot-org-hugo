July is a hot month and Foulab's air conditioners will be working
double time to support our richly diverse community. We also have some hot events 
planned.

<br/>

### No Net Night - Tuesday July 8 @ 18:30 - 23:00

Worried that wireless transmissions are influencing your thoughts?

Want to practice your conversation skills?

Tired of doomscrolling?

Wanna start a LAN party?

No Net Night returns in July, we will be turning off Internet access
for the evening and transporting people back to a simpler time.

<br/>

### Junk Swap - Saturday July 12 @ 14:00 - 16:00

Foulab will be hosting our first junk swap. 

The idea is simple: people will bring in something that is in working or
repairable condition that they no longer want, and it will be placed into
a common pile for others to claim. Anything remaining at the end will
be taken to a local recycling service such as Recyborg.

Everybody is encouraged to participate. Swing by with something old,
leave with something new (to you).

<br/>

### Project Night - Thursday July 17 @ 19:30 - 21:30

Project Night is upon us again. This is an opportunity for everybody to
talk about, demonstrate, and celebrate the projects of people in our
community.

Spectators are welcome, beginners are encouraged to present projects,
and projects may be at any stage of development. Presentations may
be as formal or informal as you'd like. We're here to have fun and
enjoy each other's efforts.

<br/>

### Foulab Monthly Meeting - Saturday July 26th @ 13:00 - 15:00

Our monthly meetings are open to the public, it is where we discuss
Foulab business and projects. One hour will be devoted to ordinary
Foulab affairs.

The second half of the meeting is devoted to a General Assembly for
our ongoing election of Foulab board officers. This time will give
an opportunity for candidates to introduce themselves and their 
platforms. The voting will commence in August.
