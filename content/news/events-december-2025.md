After an action-packed November, we will be slowing down a bit for
the holidays, but there's still a lot going on in December.

<br/>

### No Net Night - Tuesday December 2nd @ 18:30 - 23:00

It is time once again to unplug our Internet connection for the
evening. But it is all not doom and gloom. There's plenty of activities
to do at Foulab and interesting people to do them with.

And as a bonus, we will be having a lil' going away party for moti!

### Mesh Night - Wednesday December 3rd @ 19:00 - 22:00

We continue with our monthly Mesh Night. We'll be gathering to talk
about and experiment with mesh networking technologies such as 
Meshtastic and Reticulum. Newbies are also encouraged to come and learn 
about mesh networking.

Mesh Nights are a recurring monthly event, hosted on the first Wednesday of each month.

*Hosted by photon303*

<br/>

### Home Assistant Evening - Wednesday December 17th @ 19:00 - 22:00

Come and learn about automating stuff at home using Home Assistant. 
Home Assistant is so useful that we even use it in the lab to display
bus schedules, our calendar, and status updates from the laser eyes
of the bat who guards Foulab.

These nights are hosted each month on the third Wednesday of the month.

*Hosted by photon303*

<br/>

### Third SPVM Surveillance Discussion - Thursday December 18th @ 19:00 - 22:30

This will be our third meeting to discuss the SPVM's plans to introduce
AI surveillance technology.

*Hosted by m00ooo*

<br/>

### Trivia Night - Tuesday December 23rd @ 20:00 - 22:30

Our trivia night returns in December. The theme is a spin on The Christmas
Carol by Charles Dickens -- the Ghosts of Revision Control past. 

*Hosted by kida*

<br/>

### New Year's Eve Chill Night - Wednesday December 31st @ 18:30 - 23:00

We will open our doors on New Year's Eve for people who want something 
quiet(er) to do in the evening. 

<br/>

...

All December events are free and open to the public. Donations are
always appreciated.

As usual, Tuesdays and Thursdays are Open Nights. These are a great
time to visit the space for the first time. Sometimes events occur
on these nights but we are still open for socializing and other
activities. **NOTE we will be closed Christmas Day Thursday December 25.**

Mondays are our Workshop Nights. Come make things or help improve
our workshop area.

The monthly meeting and Fediverse night are on pause for the 
holidays but will resume in January.
