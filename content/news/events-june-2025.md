Another solstice graces the indomitable Foulab. As the summer days grow long, and the weather so beautiful, why not spend it inside the lab? With us? As always, all are welcome - no matter if it's your first time or your fiftieth, you'll be greeted warmly (and not just because it's summer).

<br/>

### No Net Night - Tuesday June 3 @ 18:30 - 23:00

Go local or go home: we shut off the Internet access to the Lab for one night and see what happens. Work on stuff offline, talk to another human being (???), leave (or find) a deaddrop for someone else. Poke ports and find out: the router itself (and all other local amenities) will still be operational, so even LAN parties are in the cards...

<br/>

### Mesh Night - Wednesday June 4 @ 19:00 - 22:00

Curious about alternative network topologies? Don't know what a network topology is, but ecstatic even just considering the idea of a different internet, made up of physical nodes throughout Montreal?

**kryma** hosts this edition of the (mostly) monthly night focussed on local, mesh, and other peer-to-peer networks. Come discuss, research, test, and build out peer-to-peer infrastructure from Foulab to the world (within reason).

<br/>

### Wiki Night - Tuesday June 10 @ 20:00 - 20:30

Fight entropy with your fellow Foulabbers as we write and prune and write and prune the lab wiki into shape. Some people call it lore. We call it an event.

<br/>

### Foulab Monthly Meeting - Tuesday June 17th @ 20:00 - 22:00

Prepare yourself for what some call "the Foulab meeting," where once a month we discuss all matters pertinent to ongoing lab business and projects. Only together can we keep this train rolling: your input is welcome and encouraged.

<br/>

### Killed by Dice presents: Classic Traveller - Sunday June 22nd @ 16:00 - 19:00

We have an ongoing game of Classic Traveller that we will play on June 22nd. This is an early science-fiction role-playing game. It is a mix of high tech, low tech, space exploration, trade, action, and slice of life. We have room for a few more players.

contact david AT killedbydice.com if you are interested.

Hosted by **der_moderne_man**

<br/>

### Wearable Tech Afternoon - Saturday June 28 @ 15:00 - 20:00

Obsessed with corporeally-compatible hardware? Whether it's flashing Gentoo Linux onto an arm-mounted iPod nano or assembling a whole new form of computing from spare parts, come join **kryma** and other Foulab members to talk, tinker, and build wearable technologies.

Anything goes: if you can wear it, it counts.

### Killed by Dice presents: Fantasy For Real - Sunday June 29th @ 16:00 - 19:00

In June, we will playing a one-shot of Fantasy for Real. This is a whimsical fantasy adventure role-playing game
where magic really matters. It was designed by Ron Edwards and published in 2023. Limited spots available.

contact david AT killedbydice.com if you are interested.

Hosted by **der_moderne_man**
