October is packed with events, including a Halloween celebration on
the 30th.

### Workshop Nights - Mondays @ 19:00 - 23:30

The Workshop Nights continue every Monday at Foulab. These nights
are focused on projects, especially those that may be too disruptive
for our Open Nights. 

We're also making great progress organizing the workshop area, as you
can see from the photo above. Please drop by on a Monday if you would
like to help.

*Hosted by li-ion*

### Mesh Night - Wednesday October 1st @ 19:00 - 22:00

We continue with our monthly Mesh Night. It is a perfect
opportunity to learn about and experiment with mesh networking
technologies such as Meshtastic and Reticulum. 

Mesh Nights are a recurring monthly event, hosted on the first
Wednesday of each month.

*Hosted by photon303*

<br/>

### Home Assistant Night - Wednesday October 15th @ 19:00 - 22:00

Come and learn about and tinker with Home Assistant and other home
automation technologies. 

These nights are hosted each month on the third Wednesday of the 
month.

*Hosted by photon303*

<br/>

### SPVM Surveillance Discussion - Thursday October 16th @ 19:00 - 19:84

We urgently have to take action against the [plans](https://spvm.qc.ca/en/Communiques/Details/16095) of SPVM to employ
[US American AI Surveilance Software](https://www.eff.org/deeplinks/2020/11/video-analytics-user-manuals-are-guide-dystopia) within the next few weeks.

*Hosted by adouglas89 & moti*

<br/>

### Killed By Dice Presents: Fantasy for Real - Sunday October 19th @ 16:00 - 19:00

We will play a session of Fantasy for Real on October 19. It is a
whimsical, episodic fantasy role-playing game by Ron Edwards. 

There is room for new and returning players. If you are a new player,
please visit [EventBrite](https://www.eventbrite.com/e/fantasy-for-real-rpg-event-at-foulab-tickets-1755244787409) to RSVP.

*Hosted by der_moderne_man*

<br/>

### Hacking Markets: Games of Chance - Tuesday October 21st @ 20:00 - 21:00

On the night of October 21, there will be a guest talk called 
"Hacking Markets: Games of Chance". It will be the first of a 
two-part series. 

This presentation will occur during the Open Night in our theatre room.

<br/>

### Foulab Monthly Meeting - Tuesday October 28th @ 20:00 - 21:30

Foulab meetings are where we gather to discuss Foulab business,
future plans, and provide updates on ongoing projects. 

Our meetings are open to the public so don't be dissuaded from
attending if you are not a member.

<br/>

### Fediverse Night - Wednesday October 29th @ 19:00 - 22:30

Whether you’re a developer of fediverse software, an instance admin, a
user, or just fedi-curious, join us at the monthly Fediverse Night for
discussion about decentralised social media. 

Last month's Fediverse Night was so successful that we plan to
continue hosting them on the last Wednesday of each month.

*Hosted by n00q*

<br/>

### Haunted No Net Night - Thursday October 30th @ 18:30 - 23:00

On October 30th, there will be a ghost in the machine and we will have
no choice but to turn off our Internet connection for the
evening. Don't despair. There's still plenty of activities to do at
Boo-lab during our Halloween themed No Net Night.

Please feel encouraged but not pressured to wear a costume and join
in the celebration.

<br/>

In addition to these events, we have our regular Open Nights every
Tuesday and Thursday running from 18:30 to 23:00. All of the events in
October are free to attend and open to newcomers.
