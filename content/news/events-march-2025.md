Brrr... on long cold nights such as these... it is always right to huddle next
to your local CPU for warmth and company with those in your community... Don't
miss out on all the events in this busy March.

<br/>
    
### Wednesday Mesh Evening - Wednesday March 5th @ 19:00 - 22:00

This is your sign to come join us in building the most expansive, elaborate,
decentralized, alternative network Montreal has ever seen!

The Wednesday Mesh Evening is a Wednesday evening event dedicated to mesh
networking in all its forms. From discussing it, researching and testing
alternative network topologies, to practical exercises in deploying peer-to-peer
infrastructure in FOULAB and around it.

Everyone is hosting, and **kryma** invites you to host as well. Join the network!

<br/>

### Infrastructure Night - Tuesday March 11th @ 20:00 - 22:00

Join us as we dive into FOULAB's "cinderblock" OPNsense firewall, its setup,
administration, and maintenance. 

FOULAB's Infrastructure Nights are small, bite-sized lectures and workshops on
Foulab's internal IT infrastructure - how it works, how to use it, and how to
fix things that inevitably go wrong in it.

Brought to you by **eigma**, this will be sure to increase your appreciation
of the space and what goes into maintaining it!

<br/>

### Gentoo Night - Thursday March 13th @ 20:00 - 22:00

Have you ever wondered what goes into crafting Linux installs tuned for your
specific devices and needs?

Wonder no more, and join us on March 13th as **hairu** presents [their
infrastructure](https://github.com/armtux/build-helper) for managing Gentoo
with build scripts!

### Sewing Orientation Workshop Night - Tuesday March 18th @ 20:00 - 22:00

Have a growing pile of clothes that need a bit of love, be it pants that need a
bit of a hem, or holes that you've been meaning to patch, but just haven't felt
comfortable around the arts of the fiber and cloth? 

**kida** is here to come to your rescue, hosting a workshop walking through
getting started using a sewing machine, making something simple (a pencil case?
a pouch?) and tips and tricks to get you started on your mending and making.

<br/>

### FOULAB Monthly Meeting - Thursday March 20th @ 20:00 - 21:30

This month, we'll be discussing the workshop reorganization (the light at the
end of the tunnel is there!), the museum reorganization, life at the lab, and
keeping on keeping on.

Share your ideas, your projects, and your thoughts on the future of Foulab. Your
input is important to our future!

<br/>

### Killed by Dice presents: Classic Traveller - Sunday March 23rd @ 16:00 - 20:00

We will be starting a monthly game of Classic Traveller, using the bare bones
rules from 1977/1980. The first event will include character creation and some
orientation. There are a few open spots,

contact david AT killedbydice.com if you are interested.

<br/>

### Tabletop Open Gaming Night - Tuesday March 25th @ 20:00 - 22:00

Open gaming night returns this month.

We usually play Warhammer 40K, with One Page Rules. Melee, Wizard, and Battle
Line are also available. Others are encouraged to bring games they want to
play. Warhammer? Magic the Gathering? Risk? Mouseguard: Swords & Strongholds?

<br/>

### The Roombening - Saturday March 29th @ 15:00 - 20:00

In the far year of 2025, Robots rule the world, and AI superintelligence can
generate pictures that seamlessly blend into our reality. You may have a
corporate robot in your house right now. Let's hack it and make it your
own! As roombas gain sentience, the importance of self-reliance when it
comes to hosting of infrastructure becomes more important than ever...

This event is an old-fashioned hackparty dedicated to all sorts of cleaning
robots. We'll be assuming direct control of our Roombas through hidden
maintenance ports and installing the custom firmware
[Valetudo](https://valetudo.cloud/) on any compatible machine that we find.

Bringing a cleaning robot that supports Valetudo is required if you want to
install it!
