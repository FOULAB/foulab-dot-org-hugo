February is the year's shortest longest month. Foulab has many events
this month to help pass the time.

<br/>

### No Net Night - Tuesday February 3rd @ 18:30 - 23:00

We will commemorate the first open night of February by turning off
the Internet. No Net Nights are a monthly ritual where we gather to
do activities in the lab that don't require Internet access. It is a
good time to socialize with Foulabbers, host a LAN party, check out
the retro-computing museum or our library.

### Mesh Night - Wednesday February 4th @ 19:00 - 22:00

Mesh Nights are a recurring monthly event, hosted on the first
Wednesday of each month. We will be talking about and experimenting
with mesh networking technologies such as Meshtastic and Reticulum.
Beginners are welcome to drop by, we are happy to answer your answers
and demo our devices.

*Hosted by photon303*

<br/>

### Killed by Dice Presents: Trollbabe - Sunday February 15th @ 16:00 - 19:00

On February 15th, there will be a game of Trollbabe. This is a 
table-top role-playing game about powerful women warrior-wizards.

Beginning role-players are encouraged. Pencils, paper, and dice will
be provided. If you have interested in playing, please email david AT
killedbydice DOT com to RSVP.

*Hosted by der_moderne_man*

### Foulab Monthly Meeting - Tuesday February 17th @ 20:00 - 21:30

We will discuss Foulab business during the open night of February
17th, 2026. All of our meetings are open to the public, so even
if you are not a member, you are welcome to attend the meeting and
learn how we operate. It is also a great opportunity to become
involved.

<br/>

### Introduction to Rust Programming - Thursday February 19th @ 20:00 - 22:00

Introduction to Rust. If you never tried Rust, never understood the
hype and want to discover this language: please join us. 

*Hosted by Alban*

### Faircamp Workshop - Sunday February 22nd @ 16:00 - 19:00

From Spotify running ICE recruitment ads to Bandcamp's union-busting
efforts, navigating streaming options as a musician can feel like an
ethical minefield. In this workshop we'll take matters into our own
hands by using a static site generator called Faircamp to build a
simple website where fans can browse, stream or download your music.

This workshop is geared toward total beginners, no website building
experience necessary. If you want to follow along during the workshop,
homework is available [here.](https://dognews.neocities.org)

<br/>

### Fourth SPVM Surveillance Discussion - Tuesday February 24th @ 20:00 - 22:00

This is our fourth meeting to discuss the SPVM plans to employ AI
surveillance tools. This meeting occurs on an open night so anybody
who is present is encouraged to learn about what is happening and
participate in the discussion.

<br/>

### Fediverse Night - Wednesday February 25th @ 19:00 - 22:00

Whether you’re a developer of fediverse software, an instance admin, a
user, or just fedi-curious, join us at the monthly Fediverse Night for
discussion about decentralised social media.

This is a recurring thing, hosted on the last Wednesday of each month.

*Hosted by n00q*

<br/>

### Noise Hacking Night - Friday February 27th @ 20:00 - 23:00

Friday, February 27th is a Noise Hacking Night. 

This is a night dedicated to sound synthesis and mutation, using
hardware and software. 

der_moderne_man will do a demo of his Behringer Model D Analog 
Synthesizer. If you have your own noise-makers, please bring them so
we can make noise together!

*Hosted by der_moderne_man*

All February events are all open to the public and free to attend,
although we do appreciate donations.

Tuesdays and Thursdays are Open Nights. They are a great time for 
newcomers to visit the space. Sometimes events also happen on the 
these nights.

Mondays are our Workshop Nights. On these nights people make noise
in the workshop and help with its organization.
